TVN view

The most important point of the day: the VN-Index fell for a sixth straight session to near 1,700 points, but nearly 80% of the loss came from VIC and VHM, while domestic money is not leaving the market but rotating into steel, fertilisers and private banks.

02Contextpart 3/10 · 1 min

Domestic market regulation

electronic identification of securities accounts via VNeID

TakeawayThis strengthens market quality; it is not a factor that changes the short-term price trend.

Decree 320/2026/ND-CP amending Decree 69/2024/ND-CP on electronic identification and authentication took effect on 28/9/2026; securities accounts are among the electronic transaction accounts that must be linked to and authenticated with VNeID, and accounts opened before that date must complete this by 31/12/2026. Guidance published on 12/10 requires a level-2 VNeID account or a chip ID card scanned via NFC, and the four details of name, ID number, date of birth and sex must match the National Database.

Positive impact
  • Cleans up the investor base, reducing fake and nominee accounts, in line with foreign investors' transparency demands after the upgrade.
  • Faster account opening and digital authentication; brokers with strong digitalisation gain an edge in attracting new investors.
  • Lower risk of account takeover and nominee trading, improving long-term liquidity quality.
Negative impact
  • Short-term friction around the 31/12 deadline: accounts with mismatched details must be updated before linking, which may pile work into year-end.
  • A barrier for older investors or those without a level-2 VNeID or an NFC-capable device.
  • Higher operating and customer support costs for brokers in Q4.

Sources: Decree 320/2026/ND-CP; guidance on linking securities accounts with VNeID on CafeF, 12/10/2026; KPMG Vietnam.

TVN view

This strengthens market quality; it is not a factor that changes the short-term price trend.

  • The point to watch is the actual linking pace: about 11 weeks remain to the 31/12/2026 deadline while HOSE matched value is only around VND 10,400 bn; if some retail accounts are slow to link, year-end liquidity could thin further.
  • For listed companies and brokers, the immediate task is to review shareholder and client data so the four identity details match, avoiding a December rush.

within 72 hours

TakeawayOpening the foreign ownership room is necessary but not sufficient to attract foreign capital.

Eximbank removes its 6% foreign ownership cap and brings the CEO onto the board

Source: Eximbank extraordinary general meeting 14/10/2026 via Nguoi Quan Sat, Tin nhanh chung khoan
  • Eximbank's extraordinary general meeting on 14/10 approved removing the 6% foreign ownership limit on charter capital, amending Article 20 of its charter to apply Article 63 of the Law on Credit Institutions; the board grows from 7 to 8 members with the CEO Phạm Thị Huyền Trang.
  • Chairman Nguyễn Lê Quốc Anh said this is a necessary condition required by the State Bank and expects the bank's valuation to be rated higher.
  • The financial backdrop is unfavourable: first-half pre-tax profit of VND 678.48 bn, down 54.42%, the bad-debt ratio up from 2.86% to 3.08%, and provisioning costs up 143.1%.
TVN view

Opening the foreign ownership room is necessary but not sufficient to attract foreign capital.

  • For FTSE indices, stocks are weighted by the room still open to foreign investors, so lifting the cap from 6% to the legal limit in theory raises EIB's investable weight.
  • But foreign funds buy on earnings quality, and profit down by more than half with 3.08% bad debt will make them wait at least for Q3 results.
  • For listed banks whose foreign room is still below the legal limit, this is a precedent worth following early, ahead of the next FTSE weight increase.
TP.HCM

Ho Chi Minh City consults on a municipal bond framework, comments due 16/10

Source: Ho Chi Minh City draft resolution under Resolution 222/2025/QH15, VnEconomy 07/10/2026
  • The draft allows the city People's Committee to issue municipal and project bonds in dong or foreign currency, under a medium-term programme updated annually, with buyback, swap and exchange-rate hedging operations.
  • Funds go to urban railways, transport and flood control; the urban rail strategy to 2030 alone needs about USD 25.8 - 44 bn.
  • Consultation runs 06/10 - 16/10/2026; size, tenor and coupon not yet announced.
TVN view

A new local public-debt channel will compete directly with government and corporate bonds for the same long-term money.

  • If the city issues in foreign currency, the exchange rate gains another variable.
  • First beneficiaries are infrastructure contractors, materials makers and brokers with underwriting businesses.

TVN view

The most important finding in money markets: the VND-USD overnight rate gap flipped from about minus 1.92 percentage points on average in the week of 05-09/10 to about plus 0.96 points, as the VND overnight rate rose to 4.80% against 3.84% for USD.

  • The cost of defending the exchange rate runs through liquidity: cheap money vanished just as HOSE matched value fell to around VND 10,400 bn and foreigners net sold for 16 sessions.
  • The market reads the record central rate of 25,646 dong as a bad sign, yet Vietcombank's USD selling rate fell 10 dong and the gap with the free market is only about 10 - 60 dong, meaning real pressure has not spread to the free market.
  • Marker to watch: if overnight stays above 4.5% for another week and the State Bank switches to offering bills, that signals deliberate tightening, negative for bank and broker stocks; if overnight falls back below 3%, the exchange-rate pressure has passed.

TVN view

World gold is around USD 4,160/oz, about VND 130.8 mn per tael at the Vietcombank rate, while SJC bars sell at VND 143.3 mn, about VND 12.5 mn or roughly 9.6% higher.

  • World gold has stalled because of a strong USD and the 5.215% US 10-year yield: when bonds pay 5% a year, holding non-yielding gold becomes expensive.
  • At home, SJC fell VND 1 mn but the gap with world prices remains wide, showing household hoarding demand has not cooled; this gap is one reason free-market USD demand stays high, helping push the central rate to a record.
