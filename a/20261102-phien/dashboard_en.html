TVN view

Conclusion: this was a bad session for holders but not yet a bottom; keep allocation at 40-50%, do not bottom-fish in the 03/11 session, and change the conclusion only if the VN-Index closes below 1,689.84 (cut to 30-40%) or above 1,745 with foreign investors stopping their selling (raise to 50-60%).

  • What happened: the VN-Index lost 30.90 points to 1,710.72, its steepest fall in six sessions, while HOSE matched value rose to VND 15,840 bn, the highest in 31 sessions.
  • Why: when prices break through support zones, margin investors and those sitting on losses hit sell together, like a crowd squeezing out of a narrow door; the high liquidity here is stop-loss supply meeting buy orders placed in advance at low prices, not buyers actively pushing prices up.
  • Evidence: only 46 stocks rose against 281 that fell, a 0.16/1 ratio, the lowest since the 20/7 session, and 150 stocks fell more than 2%; 27/30 VN30 stocks declined.
  • Where money left: banks (down almost 3%, VCB, VPB and BID took away 9.78 points), technology (FPT -4.74%, a more than 3-year low), retail and rubber (FRT, GVR hit the floor).
  • Where money sheltered: VIC (+1.61 points, lifted in the ATC), VJC, and a few small caps that hit the ceiling such as HDC and KOS.
  • What the market has not yet seen: the index is down only 3.0% since 26/10, but most stocks have fallen much further; the VN-Index is still 1.24% above the 19/10 low because VIC is holding it up, so the index level is masking part of the real damage to portfolios.
  • This move confirms the downtrend of the last three sessions and rejects the Morning Brief's expectation of a 1,725-1,750 range; the index closed at the bottom of the 1,710-1,725 negative scenario.
  • Watch in the 03/11 session: (1) whether the VN-Index holds 1,700 and 1,689.84; (2) if matched value drops below VND 13,000 bn while prices move sideways, selling pressure is exhausted, and only that is the first sign of a bottom; (3) whether foreign investors stop selling banks.
  • The line to remember: heavy liquidity as prices fall is the sound of sellers' doors closing, not the bell for buyers.
  • At the close 50.7% of HOSE stocks finished above their average price, better than the morning session (26.1%) and the 30/10 session (40.8%): buyers gradually stepped in during the afternoon, but only 9/30 VN30 pillars did, so the conclusion stays unchanged.
