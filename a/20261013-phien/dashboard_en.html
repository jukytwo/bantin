VN-Index falls for a fifth session

TakeawayThe most important point today: the VN-Index barely moved but the real market fell, because without the nearly 5 points from VIC and VHM the index lost about 6.5 points, and for every gainer there were more than two decliners.

  • On 13/10 the VN-Index closed at 1,719.42 points, down 1.56 points (-0.09%), its fifth straight decline; since 1,759.08 on 06/10 the index has lost 39.66 points, or 2.25%.

HOSE breadth tilted clearly to sellers with 95 advancers and 200 decliners, 112 of them down 1% or more; market-wide about 220 advancers and 420 decliners. Domestic market regulation

electronic identification of securities accounts via VNeID

TakeawayThere are 79 days left to the 31/12/2026 deadline, so compliance costs land in exactly the quarter when liquidity is weak.

Decree 320/2026/ND-CP amending rules on electronic identification and authentication took effect on 28/9/2026. Securities accounts opened before that date must be linked to VNeID by 31/12/2026, one of 12 account types that must be linked alongside telecoms, e-commerce and social media; bank accounts have until 30/6/2027. No new guidance specific to the securities industry in the past 72 hours.

Positive impact
  • Standardises investor identity, reducing fake and nominee accounts.
  • Eases account opening and digital authentication, in line with post-upgrade infrastructure.
  • Cleaner data helps monitor unusual trading.
Negative impact
  • Securities firms bear the cost of reviewing and reminding clients in Q4.
  • Possible short disruption for unlinked accounts near the deadline.
  • Older investors who rarely use apps may miss the deadline.

Source: VnExpress 11/10/2026 citing Decree 320/2026/ND-CP; not yet checked against the original on the Government portal.

TVN view

There are 79 days left to the 31/12/2026 deadline, so compliance costs land in exactly the quarter when liquidity is weak.

  • For securities firms this is like a full stock-take of the client book: laborious, but it leaves clean data, which foreign investors will demand after the upgrade.
  • The real risk falls in December, when unlinked accounts may face trading restrictions right in the year-end rebalancing season.
  • Securities firms should schedule client reminders in waves rather than leave them to year-end; listed companies should review their individual shareholder lists for the 2027 AGM season.

  • At noon on 13/10, Deputy Prime Minister Phạm Gia Túc chaired the first meeting of the Central Steering Committee on housing policy and the real-estate market after its reorganisation.
  • The Ministry of Construction was told to review all inventory; stalled projects were assigned to provincial Party secretaries to handle directly.
  • Figures cited: inventory of about 51,950 apartments and plots in Q3, trending up; real-estate credit about VND 5.23 quadrillion at end-August, over 25% of total credit; about 47,000 social housing units completed in nine months, 48% of the 97,000-unit target for 2026; the credit package under Resolution 33/NQ-CP was raised to over VND 153,000 bn but only about VND 17,500 bn has been disbursed.

Sources: Tin nhanh chung khoan 13/10 12:57; VnExpress 25/09 on the agenda of the Second Session.

TVN view

The most striking number is not inventory but disbursement: only about 11% of the social housing credit package has been disbursed, meaning the bottleneck is eligible projects, not money.

  • Real-estate credit is about a quarter of system-wide credit, 5.23 out of VND 20.75 quadrillion, so any tightening or easing here goes straight to bank asset quality; assigning stalled projects to provincial Party secretaries shortens the approval chain, like gathering files from many desks for one signature.
  • On 13/10 real estate was the support with VIC +1.37%, VHM +0.78%, KBC +1.38%, DXG +1.21%, but the market has priced in only the large caps, not social housing developers and contractors, where the second-round effect is clearer.
  • Companies with legally stalled projects should prepare files for local authorities within Q4; TVN & Partners can help prepare filings and disclosures once projects are unblocked.
  • Watch: the list of projects under review and the Second Session of the National Assembly opening 17/10 with the amended Land Law and Housing Law.

After trading on 13/10, Techcombank announced State Bank approval to raise charter capital by VND 35,730.97 bn, mainly through a 50% stock dividend, bringing expected charter capital to VND 106,593 bn.

The same day SHB signed a USD 340 mn syndicated loan led by SMBC with six international banks and received in-principle approval from the Singapore Exchange to list 3- and 5-year bonds; two deputy CEOs registered to buy 2 mn shares each from 15/10 to 12/11.

Among state-owned enterprises, Sonadezi no longer qualifies as a public company and wants to keep 99.54% state ownership until 2030. Q3 earnings season has opened: Kinh Bac posted Q3 after-tax profit of about VND 937 bn, three times a year earlier; HVS was the first broker to report, with profit of just over VND 2 bn; Hoa Phat's Q3 steel sales exceeded 3.8 mn tonnes, up 54%.

Sources: CafeF 16:44 on 13/10 (TCB), VietnamBiz 13/10 (SHB, HVS), Vietstock 13/10 (Sonadezi, Hoa Phat), VietnamBiz 12/10 (Kinh Bac).

TVN view

The TCB news is the biggest in the banking group today and not yet priced in: charter capital rises about 50%, giving the bank a thicker capital buffer for credit growth while system credit has already grown 11.59% in nine months.

  • A stock dividend does not make shareholders richer at once, like cutting a cake into more slices; the reference price will adjust on the ex-rights date, so every price level needs recalculating.
  • Notably, proprietary desks net sold VND 115 bn of TCB during the session, before the news came out, while accumulating VND 182 bn of VPB: two institutional flows choosing differently within private banks.
  • SHB shows international funding remains open to Vietnamese banks despite high USD yields, and management buying signals confidence in current prices.
  • Kinh Bac's Q3 profit tripled but nine-month profit of about VND 1,197 bn is still down 23% and only about 40% of the VND 3,000 bn plan, so Q4 must carry most of the plan.
  • Watch: TCB's dividend record date and banks' Q3 reports from mid-October.

TVN view

Dong liquidity is in surplus, not short: overnight rates are below 1% even though the State Bank has withdrawn over VND 90,000 bn in two weeks, like a tank still full after the drain valve was opened.

  • The pressure is on the exchange rate, not the dong: the central rate rose 12 dong right after the DXY hit an 18-month high, and net withdrawals are a way to reduce the dong that could be switched into USD.
  • For stocks, the paradox is that cheap interbank money is not flowing into the market, because 12-month online savings at the big banks already pay 6.8% and Sacombank quotes 7.5%, so deposits compete directly with equities; that is one reason HOSE matched value was only VND 10,862 bn.
  • UOB's refinancing-rate forecast suggests policy risk is tilted towards tightening, negative for bank and real-estate valuations.
  • Watch: whether the central rate exceeds 25,650 dong this week, and the result of the VND 17,000 bn government bond auction.
