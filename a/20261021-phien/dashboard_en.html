Electronic identification of securities accounts

Decree 320/2026/NĐ-CP, deadline 31/12/2026

TakeawayConclusion: positive in the long term, neutral in the short term; investors should complete VNeID linking before December to avoid disruption; the conclusion changes if a low share of linked accounts causes the 31/12 deadline to be pushed back.

Decree 320/2026/NĐ-CP, amending Decree 69/2024/NĐ-CP on electronic identification and authentication, was issued on 13/08/2026 and took effect from 28/09/2026: electronic transaction accounts created before the effective date in 12 sectors, including securities, must be linked to and authenticated with VNeID by 31/12/2026 at the latest; bank accounts alone have until 30/06/2027. No new documents on securities accounts have been issued in the past 72 hours.

Favourable impacts
  • Standardises investor identification, reducing fake and nominee accounts.
  • Makes opening accounts and authenticating online transactions easier, in line with upgrade criteria.
  • Securities companies with strong digital platforms benefit relatively.
Adverse impacts
  • Short disruption during the review, especially for small retail investors without level-2 VNeID.
  • Compliance and operating costs for securities companies in Q4.
  • Risk of accounts being temporarily locked if information or SIM cards are not registered in the holder's own name.

Source: Decree 320/2026/NĐ-CP (Thu Vien Phap Luat), CafeF 18/10.

TVN view

Conclusion: positive in the long term, neutral in the short term; investors should complete VNeID linking before December to avoid disruption; the conclusion changes if a low share of linked accounts causes the 31/12 deadline to be pushed back.

Risk is concentrated in the transition period around 31/12, coinciding with the transfer of nearly 300 tickers from HNX to HOSE on 28/12, so late December may see a technical dip in liquidity.

  • On 21/10/2026, the State Bank issued Official Letter 9736/NHNN-CSTT requiring commercial banks to cut the average deposit rate on new transactions with terms of 6 months or more by at least 0.5% per year in the final months of 2026.
  • The baseline is each bank's own rate in the 11-20/10/2026 reporting period, applied across all sales channels, to stop the practice of cutting posted rates while privately negotiated rates stay high.
  • The State Bank will monitor compliance through the reporting regime under Official Letter 6194/NHNN-DBTK dated 07/08/2023; banks that fail to comply may have their credit growth quotas cut or set lower.
  • The letter follows Official Letter 9608/NHNN-CSTT of 18/10, which required a cut of at least 0.5 percentage points in posted deposit rates and a 1.5-2 percentage point cut in lending rates, and the conclusions of the 19/10 meeting between Governor Phạm Đức Ấn and 46 commercial banks.
  • Things are already moving: Vietcombank cut its 12-month over-the-counter rate from 5.9% to 5.4% from 20/10, more than 25 banks have cut rates, typically by 0.5 percentage points and by up to 0.7 percentage points; VPBank is cutting lending rates by up to 1.5 percentage points with a VND 10,000 bn package from 26/10.
  • The letter takes effect immediately, with no consultation period.

Source: CafeF 21/10 15:01, VietnamPlus 21/10 15:22, Ha Noi Moi 21/10 (Official Letter 9736); Vietstock 19/10 (Official Letter 9608, 19/10 meeting); CafeF 20/10, VietNamNet 21/10 (rate schedules).

TVN view

Conclusion: this is good news for equities, especially real estate, securities firms and heavily indebted companies, but neutral to slightly negative for bank margins; favour borrowers and private banks with a high share of demand deposits, and the conclusion changes if the State Bank returns to heavy net withdrawal or the central exchange rate exceeds 25,700 dong (TVN threshold).

  • Why it matters: deposit rates are the input cost of funds for the entire system; only when the cost of funds falls by 0.5 percentage points do banks have room to cut lending rates by 1.5-2 percentage points as the Government demands, just as a shop can only cut its selling price once its purchase cost has fallen.
  • What is new compared with Official Letter 9608 is the sanction: credit quotas are what banks need most at year-end, so the threat of cutting quotas turns an appeal into an order with a price attached.
  • The market is reading the direction correctly but may be reading it too fast: real estate rose 6.13% in a single session, while lending rates have only fallen in preferential packages and most of the existing loan book has yet to benefit.
  • The checkpoints are the 12-month deposit rate schedules of the large banks over the coming week and the end-October credit report; if the average deposit rate does not fall by the full 0.5 percentage points, the real estate rally will lack a foundation.
