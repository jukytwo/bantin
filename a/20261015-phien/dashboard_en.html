The key takeaway

The VN-Index closed at 1,698.35 points, its first close below 1,700 since late July, but three stocks, VHM, BSR and GAS, took away 6.71 points, more than the index's entire 6.52-point decline, while HOSE order matching rose about 34% to around VND 14,000 bn and 212 of 366 HOSE stocks closed above their average matched price. The break of the threshold reflects a few pillars; domestic money is selectively bottom-fishing in securities, infrastructure and private banks, on the very day KOSPI lost 7.6% and the Nikkei 4.9% in a chip sell-off. TVN view

The most important conclusion today lies in this very session: the VN-Index broke 1,700 points because of three stocks, not because of the market.

  • VHM, BSR and GAS together took 6.71 points, more than the index's 6.52-point decline; strip out these three and the VN-Index would have edged up 0.19 points.
  • The mechanism is simple: the index is capitalisation-weighted, so a few large stocks falling sharply drag the whole basket down, like three heavy passengers stepping down and the whole boat sinking further even though most passengers sit still.
  • The money flow is what deserves attention: HOSE order matching rose about 34% to around VND 14,000 bn, the highest of the week, and 212 of 366 HOSE stocks closed above their average matched price, meaning buyers were willing to pay progressively higher prices into the close in securities, infrastructure and private banks.
  • What the market has not yet noticed is the decoupling from Asia: on the same day KOSPI lost 7.6% and the Nikkei 4.9% in a chip sell-off, the VN-Index lost only 0.38% and FPT even rose 1.58%, because the Vietnamese market has almost no chip stocks.
  • This does not yet reverse the seven-session downtrend, since foreign investors were still net sellers for the 17th session with about VND 23,350 bn over the streak, but it confirms that the pressure has narrowed to a few pillars.
  • For the 16/10 session, watch two levels: a VN-Index close above 1,709.54 points, today's high, and VHM holding 58,000 dong.
